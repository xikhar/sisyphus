# Generated art

Created with the built-in ImageGen tool for this game. Original PNGs and their alpha channels are preserved in `art/source/`, outside the deployed website. The body atlas is separated into individual cropped sprites by `scripts/prepare-assets.mjs` without repainting or removing alpha. `scripts/optimize-assets.mjs` makes WebP copies of the three scenery textures, retaining full-quality alpha.

Source files: `art/source/panorama.png`, `art/source/forest-ridge.png`, `art/source/limestone.png`, and `art/source/sisyphus-atlas.png`. Runtime files: three WebP textures in `public/assets/` and nine individual PNG sprites in `public/assets/sisyphus/`. Sprite crop coordinates are recorded in `art/sisyphus-crops.json`.

The fourth prompt, for the rock surface, is recorded in [TERRAIN-PROMPT.md](TERRAIN-PROMPT.md).

## panorama

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: wide 2D game environment background, 1536x1024 or wider landscape. Create an exquisite painterly panorama for a contemplative Sisyphus game: ancient Mediterranean mountain wilderness at luminous dawn, immense hazy pale blue mountain silhouettes receding into the distance, a winding sunlit river through the valley, terracotta and muted ochre rock escarpments at sides, soft sage and deep blue-green forested foothills, delicate cream clouds in pale apricot sky, one subtle pale sun high right. Sky occupies upper 45 percent, mountains middle, valley lower. Elegant traditional gouache landscape painting, fine organic paper texture, restrained atmospheric perspective, poetic and serene, subtly luminous. The whole composition should reward quiet contemplation. No people, no boulder, no building, no text, no UI. Landscape fills the entire canvas.

## ridge

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: wide transparent parallax scenery strip for a 2D Sisyphus mountain game. A continuous long undulating ridge of Mediterranean blue-green and sage conifer forest, craggy muted teal rocks, with a few tall slender cypress and sparse pine trees, a low valley in the center, taller slopes on both edges. Exquisite hand-painted gouache with organic paper texture, quiet atmospheric dawn lighting, ochre warm highlights on craggy edges, dark blue teal shadows. Wide landscape composition, ridge occupies bottom 60 percent, isolated silhouette top edge with individual trees. Actual transparent alpha background above and around the landscape, no sky, no backdrop, no checkerboard, no ground shadow, no border, no text. Bottom edge extends continuously to the bottom of the image.

## sprites

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: transparent 2D cutout skeletal animation sprite atlas. Square image, EXACT regular 3-column by 3-row grid of nine isolated objects, each centered wholly inside its own equal cell with generous 20 percent transparent padding, absolutely no overlap between cells. Hand-painted gouache, rustic ancient Greek Sisyphus, warm bronze tanned skin, dark curly hair and beard, muted ivory linen wrap, side profile facing RIGHT, dramatic warm light upper left, muted sepia shades, clean organic edges. Row 1 left: ONLY detached head and short neck in right-facing side profile, bearded Greek man. Row 1 middle: ONLY bare muscular torso from neck to waist, side view, upright vertical. Row 1 right: ONLY short ivory linen loincloth skirt hip piece with a thin dark brown tied belt. Row 2 left: ONLY one detached upper arm segment, vertical shoulder at top elbow bottom. Row 2 middle: ONLY one forearm with attached open hand, vertical elbow top fingers bottom. Row 2 right: ONLY one muscular thigh segment vertical hip top knee bottom. Row 3 left: ONLY one shin calf segment vertical knee top ankle bottom. Row 3 middle: ONLY one bare foot in side profile toes pointing RIGHT. Row 3 right: ONLY a massive approximately circular rough gray ochre granite boulder with chipped facets, illustrated rock, no shadow outside outline. Every body segment must have rounded overlapping joint ends for skeletal animation. All nine objects physically disconnected. True transparent RGBA alpha background everywhere between objects, no backdrop, no labels, no text, no grid lines, no checkerboard.
